# Manuscript figures

Every figure is provided in three formats: `.pdf` and `.svg` are the vector
originals used for typesetting, and `.png` is a raster preview. The file stems
follow the internal export names used while the manuscript was prepared, so the
mapping to the printed figure numbers is listed below.

| Figure | File stem | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Fig. 2 | `image9` | Review logic from corpus construction through the four RQ-specific evidence streams to the bounded synthesis. |
| Fig. 3 | `fig3_prisma_flow` | PRISMA-guided study-selection flow and the 108-study primary corpus. |
| Fig. 4 | `image4` | Three-dimensional classification: primary mechanism, deployment/cross-cutting facets, and the conceptual verification-evidence axis. |
| Fig. 5 | `image5` | Strongest category of reported verification evidence across 108 primary studies. |
| Fig. 6 | `image6` | Primary mechanism family by verification-evidence category (n = 108). |
| Fig. 7 | `image8` | Analytical latency projections for one illustrative explicit-certificate/ECDHE composition (P1) under assumed one-way link profiles. |

Figure 3 encodes the two-step reduction of the 304 candidate reports: 102 records
were removed as cross-source duplicates and a further 71 were excluded at title
and abstract screening, leaving the 131 reports assessed for eligibility. The
counts behind Figs. 4-6 are the same ones recorded in `../data/`, and the latency
values behind Fig. 7 come from `../performance/results/`.
