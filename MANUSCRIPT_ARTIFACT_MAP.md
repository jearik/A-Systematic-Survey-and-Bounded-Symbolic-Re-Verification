## Access boundary

The public supplementary archive contains the row-level screening, coding, analysis, model, timing, and figure artifacts described above. Frozen Web of Science and Scopus source exports are kept in the separately marked private audit bundle and should not be uploaded for publication unless the editor requests them and redistribution is permitted. The 304-record combined-source checkpoint remains arithmetically auditable rather than fully replayable as route-specific input lists.
